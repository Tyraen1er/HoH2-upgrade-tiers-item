# Upgrade Tiers Item (HoH2 Mod)

**Upgrade Tiers Item** is a mod for *Heroes of Hammerwatch II* that allows players to upgrade the tier of their equipped items at the Blacksmith shop in town.

For example, you can upgrade a *Short Bow* into a *Recurve Bow*, preserving enchantments or scaling up base stats.

---

## Features

- **Blacksmith Tier Upgrades**: Adds a new menu/option at the blacksmith in town to upgrade the tier of your equipped gear.
- **Weapon & Armor Progression**: Smoothly progress your items through tiers (e.g. Common/Low Tier to High Tier) directly using materials and gold.
- **Preserved Properties**: Upgraded items scale up their base attributes to match the new tier.

---

## How to Install (Local)

1. Copy the `upgrade_tiers_item` folder into your game's `mods/` directory:
   `...\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Heroes of Hammerwatch 2\mods\upgrade_tiers_item\`
2. Launch the game, open the **Edit Mods** menu on your profile, and enable **Upgrade Tiers Item**.

---

## How to Use

1. Enter the town and visit the **Blacksmith**.
2. Interact with the blacksmith and open the **Equipment Upgrade** tab.
3. Select your equipped item and pay the required gold and materials to upgrade it to the next tier.
